How the demo works

One page for the quarter's operations

A sample dashboard for one quarter: what each business line earned, how support tickets were resolved and how invoices were processed.

  • Revenue by business line, in € million per quarter.
  • The share of tickets resolved by an agent, an assistant or a person, month by month.
  • How 12,400 invoices were processed.

Each chart answers one kind of question

Bars compare the business lines, lines follow a change over twelve months, and the hexagon waffle shows parts of a whole: each cell is 2 % of the invoices.

Show it in four minutes

Three steps, four minutes in all.

  1. Point at the four key figures. “Savings identified, median cycle time, open exceptions and agent accuracy: the quarter at a glance.” 1 min
  2. Walk through the revenue bars. “All three business lines grew, and hardware leads in Q4.” 1 min
  3. Finish on the waffle. “Almost two in three invoices are now processed automatically.” 2 min
