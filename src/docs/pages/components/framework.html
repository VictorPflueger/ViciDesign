Framework view

A strategy framework laid out as areas, with what a team wrote in each. When agents complete, challenge and answer it, every item shows its state with a mark and a word, never with colour alone.

Areas and items

.vc-framework is a grid of .vc-framework__area sections. Each area has a title, the question it answers and a list of items. Two columns suit a SWOT, .vc-framework--3 a PESTEL; a canvas sets its own grid-template-areas on the grid. An area with nothing in it yet shows its question and a .vc-framework__empty line instead of a blank box.

An item is a details element. Its summary shows the mark, the text and the state words; opened, it shows the whole text and its notes, such as where an AI item comes from and how sure the agent is. No script is needed, and the keyboard opens it like any other details.

States

An item has a source and, once challenged or answered, a status. Its mark shows the status if there is one, otherwise the source; its words name both. Lilac marks what the AI wrote, coral what the challenge questions, sage what a chosen answer addresses, and a quiet grey what a chosen answer removes.

Areas with a state

A canvas judges whole blocks as well as items: a block whose issues reach the medium band or worse is under pressure, one with low issues only is on watch, and one a chosen answer changed is addressed. data-status="pressure", "watch" or "addressed" on the .vc-framework__area draws the coral, honey or sage edge, and a tag in .vc-framework__states under the title carries the mark and the words, such as "2 issues · high", so the state never rests on colour alone. data-source="ai" says the AI completed the area, with a lilac "AI" tag beside it.

A ring around a centre

A PESTEL looks at one company from six sides. .vc-ring puts the company in the centre and the six areas around it as flat-top hexagons, clockwise from the top, so the room sees at a glance which sides are under pressure. Each cell takes the states of an area, with the same marks and words: data-status="pressure", "watch" or "addressed" tints the hexagon coral, honey or sage, and .vc-ring__state carries the mark and the word. A cell with nothing to report shows its name and a note, such as how many factors it holds.

The order of the markup is the layout: the centre first, then the six cells clockwise from the top. The grid places them by that order, so a page under a strict security policy needs no style attribute. When a list beside the ring shows one area at a time, the cells are buttons and aria-pressed="true" marks the one shown; it takes the accent edge. --vc-ring-cell sets the height of a cell (8.5 rem by default; on phones the ring takes the width of the screen). The figure's caption names the ring and holds the area legend.

Pointing at items

When a card elsewhere on the screen is selected, such as an issue or a strategy, set aria-current="true" on the items it concerns, or on a whole area. They take the accent outline, so the room sees which items the card is about.

Tight spaces

At 1280 × 720 a framework of four areas leaves little height for each item. .vc-framework--compact shows every closed item's first line only; opening it shows the rest. The state words stay visible either way.

Check it

frameworkProblems(html) from scripts/lib/framework.mjs returns an empty list when every item has a mark and a state word, every item the AI wrote or the challenge added says so, every challenged item names its fault, every removed item says so, every area has a title, and every area with a state carries a tag with a mark and words for it. ringProblems(html) from scripts/lib/ring.mjs does the same for a ring: it has a name, its centre comes first, six named cells follow, and every cell with a state carries a mark and a word for it. Prototypes run both on each screen's markup; npm run check runs them with a self-test on this page.
