Pagination

Pages are hexagon cells, and the current page is solid merlot. Keep the first and last page visible and collapse the middle with a gap. On the first or last page, drop the href of the step that leads nowhere and add role="link" aria-disabled="true": it stays visible but can't be focused or followed.

Stepper

Shows progress through a fixed sequence. Steps are hexagon cells joined by rails: done steps are solid with a check, the current step is pastel, and upcoming steps are outlined.

Carousel

A scroll-snap track with previous/next buttons and hexagon dots. It works by touch, by trackpad and with the arrow keys on the focused track. vici.js creates the dots and keeps them in sync. Off-screen slides become inert, so keyboard focus can't land in a hidden slide.
