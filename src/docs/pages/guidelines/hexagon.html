Guidelines

Hexagon geometry

One rule gives Vici its face: anything that would be round becomes a hexagon. Anything shaped like a pill becomes a hex capsule with pointed ends. Rectangles keep small radii, so cells and capsules stand out against a calm background.

Anatomy

Vici uses regular, flat-top hexagons: flat edges at the top and bottom, points to the left and right. They line up with text baselines, stack in rows, and stretch naturally into capsules.

  • Proportion: width = height × 1.1547 (2/√3). Size a hexagon by its height and derive the width.
  • Capsule ends: the point sits 0.2887 × height in from each end. A capsule exactly 1.1547× as wide as it is tall is a regular hexagon, which is why a one-digit badge is a perfect cell.
  • Rings and focus: an outline of thickness t moves the inner corners by 0.577·t and the side points by 1.1547·t. Vici's rings use these offsets, so their edges stay exactly parallel.
  • Orientation: flat-top everywhere in the interface. Pointy-top cells are reserved for illustrations and honeycomb artwork, where the 60° lattice needs them.

Sizes

Cell sizes are heights. The width always follows from the 1.1547 ratio.

Rectangles

Buttons, inputs, cards and dialogs stay rectangular. Small radii keep them calm and let the cells carry the character. Larger containers get larger radii, and nothing is ever fully rounded: there is no radius-full token on purpose.

Recipes

Use the shape utilities to build your own cells. They work on any element, including images.

Hex capsule 
/* any element, sized by height */
.thing { height: 3rem; aspect-ratio: 1.1547; clip-path: var(--vc-hex); }

/* or in markup */
<img class="vc-hex-img" src="portrait.jpg" alt="…" style="height: 8rem">
<span class="vc-hex-ring" style="--vc-ring: 3px"></span>
<span class="vc-cell vc-cell--lg"><span class="vc-icon vc-icon--chart-bar"></span></span>

Focus on a hexagon

clip-path also clips outlines, so a hexagon can't simply use outline for its focus ring. Vici's hexagon controls draw the ring on a pseudo-element outside the shape: a 2 px hexagon ring with a 2 px gap, in the theme's focus colour. Tab to the icon button, radio or switch under Where cells appear to see it. Rectangles keep a standard outline with an offset.

Honeycomb layout

.vc-honeycomb tessellates .vc-tile elements: columns overlap by a quarter of a cell and every second column drops by half a cell. Use an even number of columns (--vc-honeycomb-cols). On phones it drops to two columns of --vc-honeycomb-cell-sm cells; set --vc-honeycomb-cols-sm: 4 to keep four columns for short labels. Tile labels shrink with the cell.
