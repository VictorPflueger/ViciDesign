Card

A card groups content about one subject. Make the whole card clickable with .vc-card--interactive and a .vc-card__link. It stretches over the card but stays a single, correctly named link for screen readers.

Accordion

Built on native <details>. Give the items the same name to let only one be open at a time. Supporting browsers animate the opening.

Which data does the agent read?

Only the sources you connect: purchase orders, contracts and supplier master data. Nothing leaves your tenant.

How are results checked?

Every finding links to its evidence and needs a human approval before anything is claimed.

Who can see the results?

Everyone in the project workspace. Reports you share by link are read-only, and links expire after 30 days.

HTML
<div class="vc-accordion">
  <details name="faq" open>
    <summary>Which data does the agent read?</summary>
    <div class="vc-accordion__content"><p>Only the sources you connect: purchase orders, contracts and supplier master data. Nothing leaves your tenant.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details name="faq">
    <summary>How are results checked?</summary>
    <div class="vc-accordion__content"><p>Every finding links to its evidence and needs a human approval before anything is claimed.</p></div>
  </details>
  <details name="faq">
    <summary>Who can see the results?</summary>
    <div class="vc-accordion__content"><p>Everyone in the project workspace. Reports you share by link are read-only, and links expire after 30 days.</p></div>
  </details>
</div>

Add .vc-accordion--boxed to put every item in its own card.

Avatar

Avatars are hexagons, with a photo or initials. Initials avatars come in the pastel families; give each person a stable colour. .vc-presence adds a status cell whose shape carries the state, not just its colour: a solid cell for --online, a half-filled one for --away, a cell with a bar for --busy and a ring (no modifier) for offline. Give it role="img" and an aria-label.

Honeycomb tile

.vc-tile is a hexagon with centred content, made to sit in a honeycomb layout. Linked tiles invert their colours on hover and focus.

Without a modifier a tile takes the soft accent colour, which turns deep wine in the dark theme. The pastel families (--merlot, --teal, --apricot, --fjord, --lilac and --honey for topics, --sage and --coral for status) stay light in both themes. --solid and --ghost complete the set.

  • Sage
  • Ghost
