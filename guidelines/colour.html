Guidelines

Colour

One deep base colour, eight pastel families and a set of linen neutrals. All ramps are generated in OKLCH, so a step number means the same visual weight in every hue. Every text and UI pairing is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on each build.

The base colour

Merlot 950 · base

#250010

oklch(0.172 0.070 357) · rgb(37 0 16)

A near-black wine. It does three jobs:

  • Ink in the light theme: headings, body text and icons (contrast 18.2:1 on the linen canvas).
  • Action: the fill of primary buttons, checked controls and the selected page in pagination.
  • Canvas in the dark theme: the page background itself, with lighter wine surfaces stacked on top.

The ramp around it is anchored on five hand-picked mood colours: #250010, #5c1230, #8f2f42, #c77d74 and the linen #f3ebe7. They are steps 950, 800, 700, 500 and neutral-100. The steps in between are interpolated in OKLCH.

Palette

Click a swatch to copy its hex value. Steps 100–300 of every chromatic family are pastels: OKLCH lightness of at least 0.83 and chroma between 0.015 and 0.10, which the build verifies.

Rules

Do
Approved Rejected AI summary

Pair status colours with an icon and a word. Meaning must survive greyscale, colour blindness and a projector.

Don't

Don't let colour alone tell the story. Three coloured dots mean nothing to a reader who can't tell them apart.

Do
Procurement Logistics Finance

For categories such as departments, projects or topics, prefer teal, apricot, fjord and blush, then lilac and honey. Coral and sage stay reserved for status.

Don't
Marketing Sales

Don't use coral or sage for categories next to status information. A red “Marketing” label reads as an error.

  • Merlot is the brand and the primary action. Use one solid merlot button per view.
  • Lilac marks anything an AI generated, suggested or summarised, plus experimental features.
  • Coral is reserved for errors, destructive actions and negative trends. It sits further towards orange than merlot, so the two are never confused.
  • Honey marks warnings and pending states. It is also the colour of filled rating cells.
  • Sage marks success, completion and positive trends.
  • Fjord carries neutral information. Teal and apricot are for categories and data only.

Proportions

A Vici screen is mostly paper. As a rule of thumb, use about 70 % linen neutrals, 20 % merlot (text and one strong action) and 10 % pastel for meaning and highlights. Pastels lose their effect when they cover everything.

The dark theme

Dark mode isn't an inverted light mode. The base colour becomes the canvas, and elevation is shown by lighter wine surfaces rather than by shadows. Text turns to linen. Primary buttons switch to a pastel blush fill with merlot text, which keeps the brand recognisable at night. Status backgrounds become the deepest shade of their family, a dark green or a dark blue, with pastel text. Alerts stay calm instead of glaring, and the colour code still reads at a glance.
