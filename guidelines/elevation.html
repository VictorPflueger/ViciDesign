Guidelines

Elevation & surfaces

Vici is mostly flat. Hierarchy comes from surface colour and hairline borders first, and shadows are kept for things that genuinely float: menus, dialogs, toasts and cards you can pick up.

Surface levels

bg-subtle · sidebars, wells

bg · the canvas

surface · cards, inputs
surface-raised + shadow-md · menus, dialogs

bg-subtle · sidebars, wells

bg · the canvas

surface · cards, inputs
surface-raised + shadow-md · menus, dialogs
HTML
<div style="padding:var(--vc-space-5);border-radius:var(--vc-radius-lg);background:var(--vc-bg-subtle)">
  <p class="vc-caption" style="margin:0 0 var(--vc-space-3)">bg-subtle · sidebars, wells</p>
  <div style="padding:var(--vc-space-5);border-radius:var(--vc-radius-lg);background:var(--vc-bg)">
    <p class="vc-caption" style="margin:0 0 var(--vc-space-3)">bg · the canvas</p>
    <div class="vc-card" style="margin-bottom:var(--vc-space-3)"><div class="vc-card__body"><span class="vc-caption">surface · cards, inputs</span></div></div>
    <div class="vc-card vc-card--elevated" style="background:var(--vc-surface-raised)"><div class="vc-card__body"><span class="vc-caption">surface-raised + shadow-md · menus, dialogs</span></div></div>
  </div>
</div>
  • Canvas (--vc-bg): linen paper in light mode, the base colour #250010 in dark mode.
  • Subtle (--vc-bg-subtle): sidebars, table headers, footers, code wells.
  • Surface (--vc-surface): cards, inputs, panels. White in light mode, a slightly lighter wine in dark mode.
  • Raised (--vc-surface-raised): anything on top of other content.

Shadows

Shadows are tinted with the base colour instead of grey, so they feel warm on linen. In dark mode they get darker and denser, because a lighter surface does most of the lifting there.

--vc-shadow-md
--vc-shadow-lg
--vc-shadow-xl
TokenUse
--vc-shadow-smSelected segment, small lifted controls
--vc-shadow-mdElevated cards, sticky elements
--vc-shadow-lgMenus, popovers, toasts, cards on hover
--vc-shadow-xlDialogs and drawers

Borders or shadows?

Do
CardHairline border

Separate static content with a 1 px --vc-border-subtle. It stays crisp in both themes and in print.

Don't
CardShadow and heavy border

Don't stack heavy shadows and strong borders on content that doesn't move. Save depth for what floats.

Layers (z-index)

Use the named layers instead of arbitrary numbers, so overlays always stack in the same order.

Layerz-index
--vc-z-base0
--vc-z-raised10
--vc-z-sticky100

--vc-z-overlay400

--vc-z-toast600

Hexagons and shadows

A clip-path also clips box-shadow. Vici's hexagon components therefore keep the host element unclipped, paint the cell on a pseudo-element, and use filter: drop-shadow() on the host when a cell must float, as the floating action button does.

HTML
<button type="button" class="vc-icon-button vc-icon-button--floating" aria-label="Create">
  <span class="vc-icon vc-icon--plus" aria-hidden="true"></span>
</button>
